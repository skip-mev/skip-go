Use this guide to migrate **USDC.n to USDC.inj** with [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/).

## Why this migration is needed

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/usdc-migration-001.png" alt="USDC.n is being deprecated in favor of USDC.inj" />
</Frame>

**USDC.n is being deprecated** and will no longer be the supported on the chains covered by this guide. If you hold USDC.n on those mentioned chains, you should migrate it to **USDC.inj**, the version that will be supported going forward.

### What are the differences between USDC.n and USDC.inj?

Both are USDC, but they originate from different chains and are treated as separate assets on Cosmos chains.

- **USDC.n:** USDC originating from Noble
- **USDC.inj:** USDC originating from Injective

---

## What happens when you migrate

After the migration, you will have **USDC.inj on the same chain where you started**.

For example:

`dYdX USDC.n → dYdX USDC.inj`

If you start with USDC.n on dYdX, you finish with USDC.inj on dYdX.

Skip:Go handles the intermediate steps automatically. You only need to sign **twice** during the migration.

## What to do

This **migration flow** enables you migrate USDC.n to USDC.inj on the same chain in one guided flow.

|    Migration Details     |                            |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| **From**                | Source chain / USDC.n      |
| **To**                  | Same chain / USDC.inj      |
| **Wallet signatures**   | 2                          |
| **Gas you prepare**     | Source chain only          |

Skip:Go handles the rest automatically.

---

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- Access to the wallet holding your **USDC.n**
- Enough gas on the chain where your USDC.n is currently held to cover the first transaction

You only need to prepare gas on your **source chain**.

You do **not** need to prepare INJ in advance. If your INJ balance is insufficient to cover the second transaction, Skip:Go will automatically recharge the required amount of INJ for you.

### Stay safe

Use only the official Skip:Go site at **go.skip.build**. Never share your seed or recovery phrase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the USDC.n you would like to migrate">
    Open [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/) and connect the wallet holding your USDC.n.

    Select:

    - **From:** The chain where your USDC.n is held / **USDC.n**
    - **To:** The **same chain** / **USDC.inj**

    For example:

    **From:** `dYdX / USDC.n`

    **To:** `dYdX / USDC.inj`

    Enter the amount you want to migrate.

    <Warning>
      Make sure the **From and To chains are the same** and the destination asset is **USDC.inj**.
    </Warning>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the first transaction">
    Review the migration details and approve the first transaction in your wallet.

    **Signature 1 of 2**

    Gas for this transaction is paid on your source chain.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait while Skip:Go processes the migration">
    Skip:Go processes the migration automatically after your first signature.

    The second signature is typically requested within about **3–5 minutes**.

    No action is needed until Skip:Go prompts you to sign again.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the second transaction">
    When prompted, approve the second transaction in your wallet.

    **Signature 2 of 2**

    You do **not** need to prepare INJ in advance. If your INJ balance is insufficient to cover the second transaction, Skip:Go will automatically recharge the required amount of INJ for you.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the migration">
    Once Skip:Go shows the migration as complete, confirm that you received **USDC.inj on the same chain where you started**.

    For example:

    `dYdX USDC.n → dYdX USDC.inj`

    **Migration complete.**

  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I use dydx.trade. Should I use this migration?">
    Only if you also hold **USDC.n directly in your dYdX Chain wallet**.

    Do not use this guide for USDC in a **dydx.trade trading subaccount**. Follow the official dYdX migration instructions for that balance when they become available.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I need to sign twice if Skip:Go handles the migration automatically?">
    **Signature 1** starts the migration from your source chain.

    Skip:Go then handles the intermediate steps automatically until **USDC.inj arrives on Injective**.

    **Signature 2** authorizes the final IBC transfer from Injective back to your **original source chain**.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I completed Signature 1, but Signature 2 hasn't appeared yet. Is something wrong?">
    After Signature 1, Skip:Go completes the intermediate migration steps before requesting Signature 2.

    This usually takes about **3–5 minutes**, but timing may vary depending on network conditions. Wait until Skip:Go prompts you to sign again.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I completed Signature 1 but not Signature 2. Where are my funds?">
    Once the automated steps after Signature 1 are complete, your **USDC.inj is on Injective**.

    If you do not complete Signature 2, your USDC.inj remains on Injective instead of being transferred back to your **original source chain**. **Your funds are not lost.**

    To move the funds, transfer your USDC.inj from Injective to the supported chain where you want to receive it.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Signature 2 asks me to sign on Injective, but I don't have any INJ. Can I continue?">
    Yes.

    You do **not** need to prepare INJ in advance. If your INJ balance is insufficient, Skip:Go provides the INJ needed for Signature 2.

    The only gas token you need to prepare is your **source chain's gas token for Signature 1**.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I selected the same chain for From and To. Why do I see Avalanche or another EVM network in the route?">
    This is expected.

    On the CCTP route, Skip:Go uses an EVM network as an **intermediate step in the background**.

    You do not need to select, fund, or interact with that network, and you do not need to prepare its gas token. Your migration still ends with **USDC.inj on your original source chain**.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is USDC.n → USDC.inj a swap?">
    Not necessarily. Skip:Go automatically determines the **best route** based on the transfer amount and the source and destination chains.

    When the source and destination chains are the **same**, amounts **above 250 USDC.n** use **CCTP as part of the migration route**.

    For other cases — including amounts of **250 USDC.n or less**, or transfers between **different source and destination chains** — Skip:Go automatically selects the best available route for the transfer.

    Regardless of the route used, you will receive **USDC.inj** on the destination chain. **No additional conversion or migration is required** on your end.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My USDC.n is on a chain that isn't supported by this migration. What should I do?">
    If your USDC.n is on a chain not listed above, check whether **Skip:Go provides a route to transfer your USDC.n to one of the supported chains listed above**. If a route is available, transfer your USDC.n to that supported chain first, then follow this migration guide.

    If no supported route is available, contact the team responsible for your chain.

    **For chain teams:** A direct IBC path with Injective is required to support your chain as a USDC.inj destination, including **IBC light clients, an ICS-20 transfer channel, and at least one reliable relayer**.

    If you need help meeting these requirements and already have a Skip contact, work with them for guidance and assistance.

    Once the IBC connection and relaying are operational, use your existing contact with Skip or submit a **Chain Integration Request** to request support for your chain as a USDC.inj destination on Skip:Go.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
