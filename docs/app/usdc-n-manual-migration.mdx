Use this guide to migrate **USDC.n to USDC.inj** with [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/).

This guide applies to USDC.n held directly on **dYdX, Babylon, Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Terra, Neutron, and ZIGChain**.

Your migration starts and ends on the **same source chain**:

**USDC.n on your source chain → USDC.inj on the same chain**

<Note>
  **Manual migration**

  - Available from **August 28, 2026**
  - **2 stages**
  - **3 wallet signatures**
  - Requires gas on the source chain, selected EVM network, and Injective

  A simpler **one-step migration is planned for September 11, 2026**. The planned flow reduces the process to **1 stage and 2 wallet signatures**, without requiring separate gas preparation on each network.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **dYdX users:** This guide applies only to USDC.n held directly on dYdX Chain. If your USDC is used as a trading balance on dydx.trade, wait for official dYdX migration instructions.
</Warning>

## Why do I need to migrate if both are USDC?

**USDC.n and USDC.inj are both USDC, but they originate from different chains.**

- **USDC.n:** USDC originating from Noble
- **USDC.inj:** USDC originating from Injective

Because they originate from different chains, **USDC.n and USDC.inj are recognized as separate assets on Cosmos chains**.

Noble plans to place its existing Cosmos chain into maintenance mode over the long term. Circle launched native USDC on Injective in May 2026, and Skip:Go is transitioning its integrations from Noble-origin USDC to Injective-origin USDC.

As part of this transition, existing **USDC.n should be migrated to USDC.inj** to use Injective-origin USDC going forward.

<Note>
  **This migration does not exchange USDC for a different asset.**

  It moves your balance from Noble-origin USDC (`USDC.n`) to Injective-origin USDC (`USDC.inj`).
</Note>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/usdc-migration-1.png" alt="USDC.n to USDC.inj is a migration between two USDC denominations, not a swap" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **This is a migration, not a swap.**

  Skip:Go may display **Swap** in parts of the interface because the same interface also supports swaps and cross-chain transfers.

  Converting USDC.n to another asset, such as DYDX, AVAX, or INJ, is a separate swap.
</Warning>

## Manual migration vs. one-step migration

|  | Manual migration | One-step migration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Availability | **From August 28, 2026** | **Planned for September 11, 2026** |
| Start | USDC.n on your chain | USDC.n on your chain |
| End | USDC.inj on the same chain | USDC.inj on the same chain |
| Migration steps | **2** | **1 planned** |
| Wallet signatures | **3** | **2 planned** |
| Gas preparation | May be required | Not required in the planned flow |

**Want to migrate manually?** Follow the manual migration guide below starting August 28, 2026.

**Prefer fewer steps?** You can wait for the one-step migration planned for September 11, 2026.

<Note>
  The three wallet signatures above are part of the manual migration itself. If you need to obtain a gas token first, additional transactions may be required.
</Note>

## Before you start

The manual migration has two stages.

**Stage 1: USDC.n on your Cosmos chain → USDC on an EVM network**

**Stage 2: EVM USDC → Injective-origin USDC → USDC.inj on your original Cosmos chain**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/usdc-migration-2.png" alt="Two-stage overview of the manual USDC.n to USDC.inj migration" />
</Frame>

This guide uses **dYdX as the source chain** and **Avalanche as the EVM network**.

If your USDC.n is on Babylon, Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Terra, Neutron, or ZIGChain, select that chain instead. The process is the same.

Skip:Go handles the intermediate cross-chain route. You do not need to manually configure CCTP, IBC, or individual bridge connections. Skip:Go can route across multiple interoperability protocols behind the scenes.

### 1. Prepare gas

The manual migration requires transactions on **three networks**:

1. Your **source chain**
2. Your selected **EVM network**
3. **Injective**

Make sure you have enough gas on all three before starting.

#### Source chain

Keep approximately **$0.10 worth of a supported fee token** on the chain where your USDC.n is held.

| Source chain | Common fee token |
| --- | --- |
| dYdX | DYDX |
| Babylon | BABY |
| Cosmos Hub | ATOM |
| Osmosis | OSMO |
| Terra | LUNA |
| Neutron | NTRN |
| ZIGChain | ZIG |

#### EVM network

Stage 2 requires gas on the EVM network you select.

This guide uses **Avalanche** as the EVM network.

| EVM network | Gas token | Approx. fee per transaction | Recommended gas balance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Avalanche** | AVAX | **~$0.01** | **~$0.10** |
| Optimism | ETH | ~$0.10 | **~$0.50** |
| Base | ETH | ~$0.23 | **~$1.00** |
| Arbitrum | ETH | ~$0.37 | **~$1.00** |
| Polygon | POL | ~$0.43 | **~$1.00** |
| Ethereum | ETH | ~$0.70 | **~$3.00** |

You can use another supported EVM network if you already have gas there or prefer to use it.

#### Injective

Stage 2 also requires INJ for gas.

| Gas token | Approx. fee per transaction | Recommended gas balance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| INJ | \~0.0025 INJ (\~$0.01) | **~$0.10 worth of INJ** |

<Note>
  Fees are estimates and may vary depending on network conditions. Recommended gas balances include additional buffer.
</Note>

If you do not have one of the required gas tokens, use Skip:Go to obtain it before starting the migration.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/usdc-migration-3.png" alt="Preparing gas on the source chain, EVM network, and Injective" />
</Frame>

### 2. Check your wallets and addresses

Before starting, confirm that:

- You control the wallet holding your USDC.n.
- You control the destination wallet on your selected EVM network.
- You control the final destination wallet on your original source chain.

This guide uses **Keplr** for the dYdX examples. For the EVM network, use a wallet supported by Skip:Go.

<Warning>
  Always select the destination address directly from a wallet that supports that network. Do not manually derive a Cosmos address from an EVM address or vice versa.
</Warning>

## Stage 1: Move USDC.n to an EVM network

Stage 1 moves USDC.n from your source chain to USDC on the selected EVM network.

This guide uses:

**dYdX USDC.n → Avalanche USDC**

- **Wallet signatures:** 1
- **Estimated time:** Under 2 minutes on Avalanche; up to about 6 minutes on other supported EVM routes

<Note>
  The screenshots use **dYdX** and **Avalanche**. If you use another supported source chain or EVM network, select your source chain and chosen EVM network instead. The steps are the same.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select USDC.n and enter the amount">
    Open [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/) and connect the wallet holding your USDC.n.

    Select:

    - **From:** dYdX / USDC.n
    - **To:** Avalanche / USDC

    Enter the amount you want to migrate.

    <Note>
      Noble-origin USDC may appear as **USDC** rather than **USDC.n** in some Skip:Go screens.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the destination address">
    Select **Set destination address**.

    Confirm that the destination network is **Avalanche**, then select the wallet that will receive the USDC.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the destination wallet">
    Connect the wallet that controls your Avalanche address.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/usdc-migration-4.png" alt="Connecting the destination wallet on Avalanche" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and sign">
    Before signing, confirm:

    - **From:** dYdX / USDC.n
    - **To:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **Amount:** Correct
    - **Destination address:** Correct

    Approve the transaction in your source wallet.

    **Signature 1 of 3**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the transfer">
    Skip:Go will track the transfer automatically.

    Keep the page open until the transfer is complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    When Skip:Go displays **Go again**, Stage 1 is complete.

    Confirm that **USDC is available on Avalanche** before continuing.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/usdc-migration-5.png" alt="Stage 1 complete, USDC available on Avalanche" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Stage 1 complete.** Continue to Stage 2 to migrate your Avalanche USDC to **USDC.inj on your original source chain**.

## Stage 2: Move EVM USDC back to your source chain as USDC.inj

Stage 2 moves your EVM USDC back to the **same source chain where the migration started**, this time as USDC.inj.

This guide uses:

**Avalanche USDC → Injective → dYdX USDC.inj**

Injective is an intermediate part of the route. Skip:Go handles this routing automatically.

- **Wallet signatures:** 2

Before continuing, confirm that:

- You have AVAX for the Avalanche transaction.
- You have INJ for the Injective transaction.
- You control the destination address on dYdX.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Avalanche USDC and dYdX USDC.inj">
    In Skip:Go, select:

    - **From:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **To:** dYdX / USDC.inj

    Enter the amount you want to migrate.

    <Note>
      If your migration started on another supported source chain, select that **same chain** as the destination and choose **USDC.inj**.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Verify:

    - **From:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **To:** dYdX / USDC.inj
    - **Amount:** Correct
    - **Destination address:** Correct

    Select **Confirm**.

    <Warning>
      **Make sure the destination asset is USDC.inj, not USDC.n.**
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Continue to signing">
    Skip:Go will notify you that **2 signatures are required**.

    This is expected. Select **Continue** and keep the window open until both signatures are complete.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/usdc-migration-6.png" alt="Skip:Go notifying that two signatures are required for Stage 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete both signatures">
    Approve both transactions when prompted:

    1. **Injective transaction** → uses INJ for gas
    2. **Avalanche transaction** → uses AVAX for gas

    Review each transaction before approving it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the migration">
    After both signatures are complete, Skip:Go will process the migration.

    Keep the page open until the migration is complete.

    Do not start another migration while the current route is still processing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    When Skip:Go displays **Go again**, the migration is complete.

    Confirm that **USDC.inj is available on dYdX**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/usdc-migration-7.png" alt="Migration complete, USDC.inj available on dYdX" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your balance is now **USDC.inj on dYdX**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I use dydx.trade. Do I need to migrate my USDC with Skip:Go?">
    **No.**

    If your USDC is used as a trading balance on [dydx.trade](https://dydx.trade/), **do not use this manual migration guide for that balance**.

    Follow official dYdX migration instructions when they become available.

    If you separately hold **USDC.n directly on dYdX Chain**, outside your dydx.trade trading balance, you can use this Skip:Go migration guide.

    | Where your USDC is held | What to do |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Trading balance on dydx.trade** | Follow official dYdX migration instructions |
    | **USDC.n held directly on dYdX Chain** | Use this Skip:Go migration guide |

    If you are unsure which balance you have, do not migrate it manually until you confirm.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my USDC.n is on a different supported chain?">
    This guide applies to USDC.n held directly on **dYdX, Babylon, Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Terra, Neutron, and ZIGChain**.

    In **Stage 1**, select the chain where your USDC.n is held.

    In **Stage 2**, select that **same chain** as the destination and choose **USDC.inj**.

    For example:

    **Osmosis USDC.n → EVM USDC → Osmosis USDC.inj**

    For dYdX, this applies only to USDC.n held directly on dYdX Chain, not your dydx.trade trading balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I need to migrate if USDC.n and USDC.inj are both USDC?">
    Both are USDC, but they originate from different chains:

    - **USDC.n:** Noble-origin USDC
    - **USDC.inj:** Injective-origin USDC

    The migration moves your balance from the Noble-origin denomination to the Injective-origin denomination that will be used going forward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is USDC.n → USDC.inj a swap?">
    No.

    The complete `USDC.n → USDC.inj` process is a **migration between two USDC denominations**.

    Converting a small amount of USDC.n to a gas token such as DYDX, AVAX, or INJ is a separate swap used only to prepare transaction fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to migrate before September 11?">
    No.

    You can use the manual migration from **August 28, 2026**, or wait for the simpler one-step migration planned for **September 11, 2026**.

    If your USDC is a trading balance on dydx.trade, follow official dYdX migration instructions instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What changes with the planned one-step migration?">
    |  | Manual migration | One-step migration (planned) |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Stages | 2 | 1 |
    | Wallet signatures | 3 | 2 |
    | Separate gas preparation | Required if needed | Not required |

    Both methods have the same result:

    **USDC.n on your source chain → USDC.inj on the same chain**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to use Avalanche?">
    No.

    Avalanche is used in this guide because it had the lowest fee and fastest completion time in our testing.

    You can use another supported EVM network if you already use it or prefer it. Make sure you have enough gas on the selected network before starting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I need gas on three networks?">
    The manual migration requires transactions on:

    1. Your source chain
    2. Your selected EVM network
    3. Injective

    Each network requires gas to process its transaction.

    If any required gas token is missing, prepare it before starting the migration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
