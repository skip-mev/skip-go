Use this guide to migrate **USDC.n to USDC.inj** with [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/).

## Why this migration is needed

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/usdc-migration-001.png" alt="USDC.n is being deprecated in favor of USDC.inj" />
</Frame>

**USDC.n is being deprecated** and will no longer be the supported version of USDC on the chains covered by this guide. If you hold USDC.n, you should migrate it to **USDC.inj**, the version that will be supported going forward.

### What are the differences between USDC.n and USDC.inj?

Both are USDC, but they originate from different chains and are treated as separate assets on Cosmos chains.

- **USDC.n:** USDC originating from Noble
- **USDC.inj:** USDC originating from Injective

---

## What happens when you migrate

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/usdc-migration-002.png"
    alt="Two-stage overview of the USDC.n to USDC.inj migration"
  />
</Frame>

After the migration, you will have **USDC.inj on the same chain where you started**.

For example:

`dYdX USDC.n → dYdX USDC.inj`

Skip:Go routes the migration through Injective. You do not need to send your funds to Injective manually.

---

## Who this guide applies to

This guide applies to USDC.n held on:

- dYdX, Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Terra, Neutron, ZIGChain

If your USDC.n is on a chain not listed above, check whether **Skip:Go provides a route to transfer it to one of the supported chains listed above**. If a route is available, transfer your USDC.n to that chain first, then follow this migration guide.

If no supported route is available, contact the team responsible for your chain.

**For chain teams:** See [What if my USDC.n is on a chain not listed above?](#what-if-my-usdc-n-is-on-a-chain-not-listed-above) in the FAQ below for the requirements and process to add support.

<Warning>
  **dYdX users**

This guide applies only to USDC.n held in your **dYdX Chain wallet**.

It does not apply to USDC in a **dydx.trade trading subaccount**. Follow the official dYdX migration instructions for that balance when they become available.

</Warning>

## Before you start

The manual migration has **two stages**:

**Stage 1:** USDC.n on your source chain → USDC on an EVM network

**Stage 2:** EVM USDC → Injective → USDC.inj on the chain where you started

This guide uses **dYdX** as the source chain and **Avalanche** as the EVM network.

If your USDC.n is on another supported source chain, select that chain instead. The overall migration flow is the same.

<Warning>
  **Stay safe**

Use only the official Skip:Go site at **go.skip.build**. Never share your seed or recovery phrase.

</Warning>

### 1. Prepare gas

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/usdc-migration-003.png"
    alt="Preparing gas on the source chain, EVM network, and Injective"
  />
</Frame>

The manual migration requires transaction fees on **three networks**:

1. Your **source chain**
2. Your selected **EVM network**
3. **Injective**

Make sure you have the required fee tokens on all three networks before starting.

#### Source chain

Keep approximately **$0.10 worth of a supported fee token** on the chain where your USDC.n is held.

| Source chain | Common fee token |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| dYdX         | DYDX             |
| Cosmos Hub   | ATOM             |
| Osmosis      | OSMO             |
| Terra        | LUNA             |
| Neutron      | NTRN             |
| ZIGChain     | ZIG              |

#### EVM network

You also need gas on the EVM network used for the migration.

| EVM network   | Gas token | Approx. fee per transaction | Recommended gas balance |
| ------------- | --------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Avalanche** | AVAX      | **~$0.01**                  | **~$0.10**              |
| Optimism      | ETH       | ~$0.10                      | **~$0.50**              |
| Base          | ETH       | ~$0.23                      | **~$1.00**              |
| Arbitrum      | ETH       | ~$0.37                      | **~$1.00**              |
| Polygon       | POL       | ~$0.43                      | **~$1.00**              |
| Ethereum      | ETH       | ~$0.70                      | **~$3.00**              |

You can use another supported EVM network if you prefer or already have gas there.

#### Injective

You need **INJ** to pay the Injective transaction fee in Stage 2.

| Gas token | Approx. fee per transaction | Recommended gas balance |
| --------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| INJ       | \~0.0025 INJ (\~$0.01)      | **~$0.10 worth of INJ** |

<Note>
  Fees are estimates and may vary with network conditions. Recommended balances include extra
  buffer.
</Note>

### 2. Check your wallets and addresses

Before starting, make sure you can access:

- The wallet holding your **USDC.n**
- A wallet on your selected **EVM network**
- An **Injective address with INJ** for gas
- An address on your original source chain to receive **USDC.inj**

Your source and destination addresses may be in the same wallet, and the same wallet may support more than one of these networks.

This guide uses **Keplr** for dYdX and Injective. For the EVM network, use a wallet supported by Skip:Go.

<Warning>
  Select each address from a wallet that supports that network. Do not manually convert one address
  format into another.
</Warning>

## Stage 1: Move USDC.n to an EVM network

**dYdX USDC.n → Avalanche USDC**

- **Wallet signatures:** 1
- **Estimated time:** Up to 6 minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select USDC.n and enter the amount">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/usdc-migration-004.png"
        alt="Selecting USDC.n as the source asset and entering the migration amount"
      />
    </Frame>

    Open [Skip:Go](https://go.skip.build/) and connect the wallet holding your USDC.n.

    Select:

    - **From:** dYdX / USDC.n
    - **To:** Avalanche / USDC

    Enter the amount you want to migrate.

    Select **Swap** to continue.

    <Note>
      Skip:Go uses its standard **Swap** interface here, even though this step is part of the USDC.n migration.
    </Note>

  </Step>

<Step title="Set the destination address">Select **Set destination address**.</Step>

  <Step title="Connect the destination wallet">
    Select the wallet that controls your Avalanche address.

    Confirm the destination address before continuing.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and sign">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/usdc-migration-005.png" alt="Reviewing the Stage 1 transaction before signing" />
    </Frame>

    Check:

    - **From:** dYdX / USDC.n
    - **To:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **Amount**
    - **Destination address**

    Approve the transaction in your source wallet.

    **Signature 1 of 3**

  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the transfer">
    Skip:Go will track the transfer automatically.

    **Keep this page open** while the transfer is in progress.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Stage 1">
    When Skip:Go displays **Go again**, Stage 1 is complete.

    Your USDC is now on Avalanche.

    Continue to **Stage 2** to migrate it to **USDC.inj on your original source chain**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Stage 2: Move EVM USDC to USDC.inj on your source chain

**Avalanche USDC → Injective → dYdX USDC.inj**

- **Wallet signatures:** 2

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select USDC and USDC.inj">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/usdc-migration-006.png"
        alt="Selecting Avalanche USDC as the source and dYdX USDC.inj as the destination"
      />
    </Frame>

    In Skip:Go, select:

    - **From:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **To:** dYdX / USDC.inj

    Enter the amount you want to migrate.

    If your migration started on another supported source chain, select that **same chain** as the destination.

    <Warning>
      Make sure the destination asset is **USDC.inj**, not USDC.n.
    </Warning>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check:

    - **From:** Avalanche / USDC
    - **To:** dYdX / USDC.inj
    - **Amount**
    - **Destination address**

    Select **Confirm**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Continue to signing">
    Skip:Go will display **2 signatures required**.

    Select **Continue** and keep the window open until both signatures are complete.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete both signatures">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/usdc-migration-007.png"
        alt="Approving both required signatures to complete Stage 2"
      />
    </Frame>

    Approve both transactions when prompted:

    1. **Injective transaction** → uses INJ for the transaction fee
    2. **Avalanche transaction** → uses AVAX for the transaction fee

    Review each transaction before approving it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the migration">
    After both transactions are approved, Skip:Go will process the migration.

    **Keep this page open** while the migration is in progress.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    When Skip:Go displays **Go again**, the migration is complete.

    Confirm that **USDC.inj is available on dYdX**.

    **Migration complete:**

    `dYdX USDC.n → dYdX USDC.inj`

  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I use dydx.trade. Should I use this guide?">
    Only if you also hold **USDC.n directly in your dYdX Chain wallet**.

    Do not use this guide for USDC in a **dydx.trade trading subaccount**. Follow the official dYdX migration instructions for that balance when they become available.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I stop during the manual migration?">
    You can resume the migration later.

    - **After Stage 1:** Your USDC remains on the EVM network you selected. You can start Stage 2 later using that USDC.
    - **During Stage 2 signing:** Both signatures are required before Stage 2 proceeds. If you stop before completing both signatures, your USDC remains on the EVM network. You can start Stage 2 again when you are ready.

    For example, if you use Avalanche, your USDC remains on **Avalanche** until Stage 2 proceeds.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is USDC.n → USDC.inj a swap?">
    This guide is specifically for migrating your balance from **USDC.n to USDC.inj**.

    Skip:Go may display **Swap** in parts of the interface because the same interface is also used for swaps and cross-chain transfers.

    Swapping USDC.n for another token, such as DYDX, AVAX, or INJ, is a separate transaction and is not part of this migration.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to migrate before mid-September?">
    No. **Mid-September is the planned launch date for the one-step migration, not a migration deadline.**

    Manual migration will be available starting **August 28, 2026**. You can use the manual flow or wait for the one-step migration planned for mid-September.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to use Avalanche?">
    No.

    This guide uses Avalanche as an example; you can use any supported EVM network.

    If you choose another network, make sure you have the required gas token before you start.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Injective - EVM and Injective - Cosmos?">
    Injective - EVM and Injective - Cosmos are the same networks but **they use different transfer routes**.

    To follow this CCTP migration guide, please select **Injective - EVM** that uses the CCTP route.

    Already completed the transfer using **Injective - Cosmos**? That's okay. Skip:Go uses a swap route instead of CCTP to ensure that you will still receive **USDC.inj**. If the transfer successfully completed, no further migration is needed.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/usdc-migration-008.png"
        alt="Injective - EVM and Injective - Cosmos are the same network with different transfer routes"
      />
    </Frame>

  </Accordion>

<div id="what-if-my-usdc-n-is-on-a-chain-not-listed-above" />

  <Accordion title="What if my USDC.n is on a chain not listed above?">
    If your USDC.n is on a chain not listed above, check whether **Skip:Go provides a route to transfer your USDC.n to one of the supported chains listed above**. If a route is available, transfer your USDC.n to that supported chain first, then follow this migration guide.

    If no supported route is available, contact the team responsible for your chain.

    **For chain teams:** A direct IBC path with Injective is required to support your chain as a USDC.inj destination, including **IBC light clients, an ICS-20 transfer channel, and at least one reliable relayer**.

    If you need help meeting these requirements and already have a Skip contact, work with them for guidance and assistance.

    Once the IBC connection and relaying are operational, use your existing contact with Skip or submit a **Chain Integration Request** to request support for your chain as a USDC.inj destination on Skip:Go.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
